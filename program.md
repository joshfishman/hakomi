## Program Attendance Expectations to receive a Certificate of Completion Authorizing the Teaching of Loving Presence

Attend 8 3-day weekend sessions totaling 24 days.
Attendees may use additional Thursday Individual Therapy Sessions days to make up missed weekend sessions.


## Program Expectations to become a Certified Refined Hakomi Practitioner

- Attend 8 3-day weekend sessions totaling 24 days.
- Attendees may use additional Thursday Individual Therapy Sessions days to make up missed weekend sessions.
- Participate in the Apprenticeship Group after interviewing with Marina where both participant and Marina will create a plan for certification.
- The purpose of the Apprenticeship Group, integrated into the Comprehensive Training Program, is designed to allow participants to gain the experience necessary to demonstrate their competence in the Refined Hakomi method for certification as a Hakomi Practitioner.
